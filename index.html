ABOUT OKUHLE PETROLHEADS

OKUHLE PETROLHEADS is an automotive information website created for South African car enthusiasts. It brings together information about popular vehicle brands, vehicle categories, engineering, performance and everyday motoring.

Whether you love hatchbacks, luxury sedans, sports cars, SUVs, bakkies or off-road vehicles, this is your place to explore the automotive world.
